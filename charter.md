# ARMOR Research Group (IRTF) - Proposed Charter v0.1

## Mission

Advance the technical resilience of Internet protocols in adversarial or
disrupted networks, ensuring dependable end-to-end connectivity.

## Scope

- Empirical measurement of interference and protocol behavior.
- Design, analysis, and evaluation of adaptive techniques (e.g., overlay
  routing, multi-path, polymorphism, traffic obfuscation, tunneling)
- Deployment studies for emerging mechanisms (e.g., ECH, MASQUE); theory
  that informs practice.

## Non-Goals

Policy advocacy; standards-track protocol development; product promotion;
real-time incident response.

## Outputs

IRTF-stream Informational or Experimental documents (surveys, evaluations,
best practices), plus shared methods, datasets, and testbeds where useful.

## Coordination

Loose alignment with PEARG (privacy), MAPRG (measurement), HRPC
(human-rights considerations), and GAIA (access).

## Participation

Open to researchers, engineers, and operators from academia, industry, and
civil society.

## Meetings

ARMOR meets 1-3 times per year, with at least one meeting co-located with an
IETF meeting; sessions are open, recorded when possible, and support remote
participation.
